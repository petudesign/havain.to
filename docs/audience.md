# For whom

"For whom" is two different questions. Keep them apart.

## 1. Users of the tool

| Who | What they need | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Store employee doing date checks | Know which shelf sections to check now; see what the previous shift already did | Primary user. Works on a handheld scanner or phone, often in short slots between other tasks. |
| **New or temporary employee** | Do the task right without knowing the store | **Design target** ([design-principles.md](design-principles.md), P1). High turnover; may come from another store size or chain, or have no retail experience. |
| **Employee with a disability or special needs** | A clear, calm task they can complete on their own | Must not be excluded (P2). Often a narrower role, e.g. labelling only; decisions stay with a shift lead. |
| Shift lead | See whether the checks got done; plan time | Secondary. |
| Store / chain | Less waste, more recovered value, less time spent | Buyer. Measured in euros and hours. In Kesko's model the individual K-retailer runs the store and makes this decision. |
| Customer | Find discounted near-expiry food | Indirect in this project. The 2025 concept covered the in-store point and app; this project focuses on the shelf work. |

To fill in from interviews: how checks are actually split between shifts, what device is used, how much time a check gets in practice.
