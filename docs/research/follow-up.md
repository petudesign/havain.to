# Research follow-up questions

Raised 2026-11-10 after the first report. Can be given to the research agent with the same rules and output format as [brief.md](brief.md).

**F1. Staff turnover in Finnish grocery retail.** Turnover rate, share of part-time and temporary staff, typical length of employment, share of staff with Finnish as a second language. Sources: Kaupan liitto, PAM, Statistics Finland, chains' annual or sustainability reports.

**F2. Employees with disabilities in Finnish grocery stores.** Public examples and programmes from Kesko, S-Group, Lidl and others (e.g. employment of people with intellectual disabilities, supported employment, work trials). What tasks do they typically do? Any published guidance on accessible work tools in retail.

**F3. Cognitive accessibility guidance.** Current W3C guidance for cognitive and learning disabilities (COGA, "Making Content Usable for People with Cognitive and Learning Disabilities"), relevant WCAG 2.2 success criteria, and any guidance on accessible tools for workplaces rather than consumer websites.

**F4. Category profiles beyond dairy.** Typical remaining shelf life on delivery and waste rate for meat, fresh bread and deli products in Finnish or Nordic retail. Note: report D11 found nothing for dairy; say "not found" again if so.

**F5. Store-size differences.** How date checks and staffing differ between hypermarkets, supermarkets and neighbourhood stores (any public material from Kesko, S-Group, Lidl or research).

**F6. Onboarding in grocery stores.** How new employees are trained on date checks and rotation; how long onboarding takes; whether chains use digital task tools for it.

**F7. Electronic shelf labels in Finnish grocery stores.** How widespread are ESLs (Kesko, S-Group, Lidl), which store types, any public rollout figures. Are ESL prices already changed automatically for near-expiry products anywhere in the Nordics?

**F8. Price-marking rules.** Finnish rules on price marking (shelf vs checkout price), and on how a near-expiry discount must be displayed. Do they allow automatic ESL price changes during the day? Sources: Finlex (hintamerkintäasetus), Finnish Competition and Consumer Authority (KKV).

**F9. Saarinen (Aalto) study in detail.** Find the original paper ("Reaping the Benefits of Expiration Tracking" or similar): setting (online grocery), method, how the automatic discount worked, the 9.6–10 % and 15 % figures, and whether it says anything about physical stores or ESLs.
